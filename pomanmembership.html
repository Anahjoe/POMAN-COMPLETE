Frequently Asked Questions

What is #POMAN? ▼

Peter Obi's Movement for an Authentic Nation - building authentic leadership and governance in Nigeria through grassroots mobilization and competence-based leadership.

How do referrals work? ▼

Share your unique referral code with friends. Each person who joins using your code earns you points. Different tiers earn different points (Supporter=1, Active=10, Ambassador=25, Founder=50). Collect points to unlock exclusive badges and rewards!

What are the badge benefits? ▼

Community Builder (10 pts): Profile badge, WhatsApp access, 10% discount. Movement Champion (50 pts): Priority events, 1-on-1 with leadership, 20% discount. Elite Recruiter (100 pts): FREE Ambassador upgrade, Leadership Council, 30% discount. Legendary (250+ pts): Lifetime FREE Ambassador, Co-Founder status, 5% revenue share!

Can I upgrade my membership? ▼

Yes! You can upgrade anytime. Contact us at pomanmovement@proton.me or WhatsApp +234 702 661 5694 to upgrade your membership tier.

How long is membership valid? ▼

Supporter: Ongoing (free). Active Member & Ambassador: 1 year (renewable). Founder Member: Lifetime. All memberships include full access to community benefits during the valid period.
